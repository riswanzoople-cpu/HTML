A mental wellbeing platform designed to make emotional support more accessible, personal and approachable.

Naam is an AI-first mental wellbeing application designed to help individuals understand their emotions, access appropriate support and build healthier daily habits. The platform combines AI-guided emotional support, connections with independent mental health professionals and everyday wellness resources in one place. Through a simple and empathetic experience, Naam aims to make taking care of mental wellbeing feel less overwhelming and more accessible.
